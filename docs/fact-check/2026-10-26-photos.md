# Photo fact-check — 26 October 2026

Read-only check of lead photos and interest-story images. No island data was edited. `scripts/verify_island_images.py` was not re-run and `--fix-suspect` was not used. Judgements below come from the Commons file page (title, description, categories, and coordinates or wikitext when the description was thin), read on 26 October 2026. Image binaries were not saved into the repo.

A low automated score is not treated as a confirmed error. A confirmed error names the island id, the page that was read, what the atlas implies, and what that page says the file shows.

### Whisky

All 22 pictures that visitors can see name the distillery, or sit on it.

Scapa’s panoramio file is in category “Scapa distillery”, at about 58.96°N, 2.97°W, which is the distillery by Scapa Flow, not the flow alone. Bruichladdich’s `Distilleryreflection.jpg` is in category “Bruichladdich distillery”. Port Charlotte’s picture is titled as the disused distillery, which is that site. Not confirmed as a wrong place.

Eight whisky islands have stories and no story image, so there was nothing to judge: Barra, Kimbland, Shetland (Lerwick), Isle of Man, Eriskay, Thorne Island, Nun’s Island, Anglesey.
