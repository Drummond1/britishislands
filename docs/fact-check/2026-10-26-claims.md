### Tidal islands (64 of 66)

Every one of the 66 names appears on https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_island. The “joined to” phrases match that list, including the pairs that are easy to mix up: Portmuck rather than the Small Isles Muck; Oronsay with Colonsay and the separate Oronsay in Loch Bracadale; Northmavine Uyea rather than the Unst one; Tenby’s St Catherine’s Island; the Furness Sheep Island; Sligo’s Coney Island; Bernera off Lismore rather than Great Bernera; White Island with St Martin’s, Scilly. Eilean Tigh is a real tidal island, joined to Raasay by a ledge at low tide. Scolt Head can be reached on foot at low tide; the list includes it. Ynys Feurig is a set of islets off Anglesey that dry out towards the shore. Baleshare and Mahee are the two where the “covered at high tide” sentence is false.

Pennsylvania Castle was in the castle sample and held: the article calls it a Gothic Revival mansion in Wakeham on the Isle of Portland. The sentence is true of Portland.
