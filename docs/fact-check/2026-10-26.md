Read-only. `data/islands.json` and `data/interest_index.json` were not edited.
Four checks ran in parallel. Detail sits in the linked reports. This page is
the visitor-facing summary.

A spot-check against the files confirmed Inner Farne, Davaar, Knightstone,
and Glashedy carry the wrong nation; Hayling’s stored area is 16.2 km²;
Skye’s population is 10,008 labelled census 2022; Highland Park’s summary
still says it is the northernmost single malt; Airds Castle’s summary places
the ruin on Kintyre and the row is filed on Arran; La Boue’s lead file is
titled as the Palmarin reserve.

## What held

- Of 2,559 named islands with a pin and a cached Wikidata point, 2,549 sit
  within 5 km. The ten farther cases were opened and were not confirmed as
  wrong pins.
- The 27 curated islands are on the right feature. The validation set’s
  type and nation rows match.
- Great Arthur House and the Thorney Island school are already gone.
  Cardigan Island’s area is 0.15 km², so the old 40 km² note is stale.
- Whisky: 53 of 57 sentences match the cited page, including Laggan Bay
  opening on 2 April 2026, Anglesey Môn near Pentraeth, Nun’s Island as a
  historical Galway still, and Barra’s gin still with a planned whisky
  still. Achill is absent, which is right.
- Wildlife: 29 of 30 island-level presence claims held.
- Tidal islands: all 66 names are on the Wikipedia tidal-island list. Two
  of the “covered at high tide” lines are false.
- Whisky story photos matched their distilleries.

## Wrong nation (156)

`nation_for()` in `scripts/fetch_islands.py` uses overlapping boxes.
Scotland’s box takes Northumberland, the Farnes, and five Cumbrian islets.
Northern Ireland’s box takes Donegal (including Glashedy and Inch in Lough
Swilly), Lough Allen, Garadice, Lough Oughter, Lough Eske, and the Leitrim
side of Lough Melvin. It also takes Davaar, Sanda, Sheep Island, Glunimore,
and Paterson’s Rock, which are Scotland. Wales’s box takes Knightstone,
Birnbeck, Steep Holm, and Langford Grounds, which are England. Five Pembrokeshire
features (Grassholm, the South Bishop, and three neighbours) are tagged Ireland.

Flat Holm, Denny Island, Lindisfarne, Hestan, and Rough Island at Kippford
were checked and are already right. Do not flip those.

## Not an island

- Greenisland (`csv-geocoded-Q1375188`) is the town on Belfast Lough, filed
  as a sea island in England. It is in County Antrim.
- Island Magee (`wd-Q24639396`) is a peninsula, filed as sea. The nation,
  Northern Ireland, is right.
- Oxford (`thames-oxford`) is a pin in the city centre, filed as a river
  island. Oxford Castle is attached to it. The castle stands in the city.

## Sentences

Castles on the wrong island: Airds Castle (on Arran; it is on Kintyre),
Torksey Castle (on Torksey Island; it is in the village), Calvay Castle
(on Calbhaigh; it is on a neighbouring islet), Cloughoughter Castle (on
Stony Island; it has its own island in Lough Oughter). Pennsylvania Castle
on Portland held.

Whisky: Highland Park is no longer the northernmost single malt, and Scapa
is not the third-northernmost. Lerwick and Kimbland on Sanday are farther
north. The rest of the whisky list held.

Wildlife: the Alderney hedgehog sentence reads as if the species is rare.
The source means the blonde colour form. Alderney is a real home of that
form.

Tidal: Baleshare’s link is a 1962 road causeway. Mahee’s causeways are
roads usable at high tide. The shared “covered at high tide” line is false
for both.

Film and music, in a 20-row sample of likely collisions: the Great Silkie
is about Sule Skerry, not Flotta; King Orfeo’s summary is the Middle
English poem, not a Shetland song; an “Ailsa Craig” text on Arran describes
the island of Ailsa Craig; several MusicBrainz rows paste a famous
namesake (Peter Pratt, Julian Beale, Neil Ferguson, and others) onto
Orkney, Islay, North Uist, or Burray.

For sale, 8 broker pages opened: Insh Island and Gasker are marked sold
and are still in the list of 42. Kerrera’s ferry house is under offer.
Two pages did not load.

Curated prose: Belle Isle’s round house is Grade I, not Grade II*. The
other 26 curated descriptions held at the level of nation, archipelago,
and summit.

  `medium`.
- Mainland Orkney, 580.6 km². The island is 523.25 km². The extra matches
  the Churchill Barrier isles, which have their own records. `curated.json`
  still has 523.
- Summits: Arranmore 281 m (about 227–229), Cape Clear 213 m (160), Bere
  320 m (270), Inishmore 71 m at An Charcair (An Droim Rua, 123 m), Tresco
  34 m at Gun Hill (Castle Down, 44 m), Sùla Sgeir 305 m (252), Lundy
  138 m at Tibbett’s Hill (Beacon Hill, 143 m).
- Population labelled census 2022 stores the 2011 island total: Skye
  10,008 (2022: 10,496), Lewis and Harris 21,031 (19,680), Islay 3,498
  (3,180), Arran 4,679 (4,618). Fair Isle is 55 against a 2022 figure of
  44.
