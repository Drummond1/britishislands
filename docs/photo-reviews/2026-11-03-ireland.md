The user authorised publication of the reviewed photo corrections and additions
to findmyisland.com. Scope covers Ireland and Northern Ireland separately.

The 108 additions comprise nine new covers, 13 ordinary gallery additions and
86 captioned supporting views. Placements and distinct photographs differ
because some panoramas correctly illustrate more than one island, and one
previously used photo gained a second verified association. An existing
Inishgort photo was restored/promoted, not counted as newly introduced.

The 273 removals comprise 16 canonical photo associations, 245 gallery
associations (including seven rights quarantines), and 12 story illustrations.
Confirmed errors include West Maiden's lighthouse used for East Maiden,
mainland scenes, unrelated namesakes, maps, logos and unsuitable animal closeups.
East Maiden now uses a verified Oliver Dixon Geograph image; Bull Island gains
a verified William Murphy Flickr dune photograph. Source pages, authors and
open licences are retained for every adopted photo.

**382 existing associations remain unresolved and unchanged**: 78 canonical,
303 gallery and one story illustration. They are not certified as correct.
One additional Bull Island aerial candidate was withheld for inadequate
third-party permission evidence and was never adopted.

## Evidence and method

The [exact change ledger](2026-11-03-ireland-changes.json) contains all reviewed
changes, previous/replacement image records, source links, reasons and reviewers.
Local discovery caches and original-aspect visual inspections are retained in
the project audit workspace, rather than included in the public release.

The first audit examined all 1,403 existing island–image associations, with
pixels available for 1,401. Discovery and final acceptance were assigned to
different agents. The extended search covered 430 distinct coastal/inland/NI
targets through Commons, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage and native Geograph, with
additional Flickr and institutional checks. Cached searches and failures were
not represented as fresh successful searches. All 90 extended-round candidates
received separate verification; 89 were accepted, one withheld. Eight proposed
covers were downgraded to context and several credits corrected from exact-file
evidence, including Eckhard Pecher / Patrick Charpiat for an Inisheer photograph.

Identity checking combined the exact source description, island/county/lake
context and visible subject. A matching name, camera position, nearby search
result or Wikipedia association alone was insufficient. Flickr and Geograph
originals were reconciled with renamed Commons copies using exact source links.
Aerial views use modest derivatives, and prominent identifiable people and
misleading non-photographs were excluded.

Supporting photographs use `contextOnly: true`, with explicit captions and
credits under “Views and landmarks”. They cannot become covers, thumbnails or
failed-cover fallbacks. Reviewed story overrides are tied to the exact story
title and source photo, preserving future corrections using different images.

## Validation and publication

All 11,212 islands and 27 curated records are retained. Non-photo canonical
fields, non-Irish records and unrelated changes are preserved. The final
dataset has 404 Irish/NI canonical image associations, 768 gallery associations
and 66 story images; 2,792 atlas records have canonical photographs.

Compact index, seven nation shards and supported detail files are rebuilt for
deployment. There are 11,183 supported detail filenames and 29 existing
non-ASCII exclusions using shard fallback. The geographic regression remains
18/19 with the pre-existing Wallasea parent-water mismatch. All ten focused checks pass for
context-only images, missing/failing covers, mixed galleries and visible credits.
An independent release reviewer reconciled the full ledger and verified that
all 77 source-specific story overrides regenerate correctly and are idempotent.
The production artifact build and 11,183 generated detail comparisons pass.

The publishing session verifies the GitHub Pages run and live photo data after
the release commit; preparing this document does not itself claim deployment.
