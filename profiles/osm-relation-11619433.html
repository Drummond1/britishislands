Stac Soay

Stac Shoaigh (Scottish Gaelic: "stack of Soay") is a sea stack rising 62 metres (203 ft) from the Atlantic Ocean, located between the islands of Hirta and Soay in the St Kilda archipelago in Scotland. It is positioned east of the taller Stac Biorach.
