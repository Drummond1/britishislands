Northern Ireland

Lusty More

Lustymore Island (from Irish Loiste Mór 'big lodge') is an island located in Lower Lough Erne, County Fermanagh, Northern Ireland. Nearby is Boa Island whose Caldragh cemetery has a carved Janus-type figure.
