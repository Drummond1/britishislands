Great Saltee

The Saltee Islands (Irish: Na Sailtí; Old Norse: Saltey) are a pair of small islands lying 5 kilometres off the southern coast of County Wexford in Ireland. The two islands are Great Saltee (89 ha (0.89 km2)) and Little Saltee (37 ha (0.37 km2)), with a combined area of 126 ha (1.26 km2).
