Inchcailloch

Wooded island in Loch Lomond, part of the Loch Lomond National Nature Reserve, with a circular trail and the ruined chapel of St Kentigerna.

Names

Scottish Gaelic: Innis na Cailleach; Irish: Innis na Cailleach

Geography

Mostly oak woodland; one of the inspirations for Loch Lomond National Park's conservation focus.

Geology

Bedrock: Stratheden Group (Sandstone And Conglomerate, Interbedded), Late Devonian–Late Devonian. Superficial deposits: Superficial Deposits Not Mapped [For Digital Map Use Only] (Rock (Undifferentiated)). History

Site of an 8th-century nunnery dedicated to St Kentigerna. The MacGregor clan burial ground remains in use.

How to reach it

Passenger ferry from Balmaha; canoe and kayak hire also available locally.

Accommodation

Wild camping permitted at the designated Port Bawn site (permit required March–September).
