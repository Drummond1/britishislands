Guernsey

Second-largest Channel Island, with the picturesque harbour of St Peter Port and a strong maritime tradition.

Names

Irish: Geansaí; Welsh: Ynys y Garn; Cornish: Gernsi; French: Guernesey

Geography

Higher and more cliff-bound in the south, lower and beachier in the north. The Bailiwick also includes Sark, Herm and Alderney.

History

Part of the Duchy of Normandy from 933. Occupied 1940–1945 — Castle Cornet's German fortifications still stand.

How to reach it

Condor Ferries from Poole and Portsmouth; flights into Guernsey Airport from across the UK and Europe.

Accommodation

Hotels, guest houses and self-catering across the island, concentrated around St Peter Port.
