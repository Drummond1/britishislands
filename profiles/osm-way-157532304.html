South Ascrib

South Ascrib is an island in Loch Snizort between the Waternish and Trotternish peninsulas on the northern coasts of the island of Skye in Scotland. At low tide, South Ascrib extends over roughly 30 hectares (74 acres), and its highest point is 41 metres (135 ft) above sea level.
