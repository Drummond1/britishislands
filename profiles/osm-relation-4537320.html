Eilean an Taighe

"Eilean an Taighe", or "Eilean Taigh" is a fairly common island name Eilean an T(a)ighe, meaning "House Island" (lit. "Island of the House"), is one of the Shiant Islands. It is joined to Garbh Eilean by an isthmus, so they each form part of what is actually a single island.
