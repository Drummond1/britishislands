Nun's Island

Nun's Island (sometimes Nuns Island; Irish: Oileán Ealtanach / Altanach, meaning ' island of the flock of birds"') is an island on the River Corrib in Galway city, and an electoral division, close to Galway Cathedral. It is the location of St Joseph's Patrician College ("The Bish"), Nuns Island Thea
