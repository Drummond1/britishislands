Flodday

Flodday (Scottish Gaelic: Flodaigh) is an uninhabited island in Loch Maddy, North Uist in the Outer Hebrides of Scotland. The area of the island is recorded as 50 hectares (124 acres) in Rick Livingstone's tables, although it is not listed by Hamish Haswell-Smith in his tabulation of Scottish island
