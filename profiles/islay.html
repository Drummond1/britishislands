Islay

The 'Queen of the Hebrides', world-famous for its peated single malt whiskies and rich birdlife.

Names

Scottish Gaelic: Ìle; Irish: Ìle; Welsh: Ìle; Also known as: Ìle

Geography

Lower-lying than its neighbours, with extensive peat moor, sea lochs and beaches. Home to over 100 species of breeding birds.

Geology

Bedrock: Appin Group (Graphitic Pelite, Calcareous Pelite, Calcsilicate-Rock And Psammite), Neoproterozoic–Neoproterozoic. Superficial deposits: Alluvium (Clay (Undifferentiated) And Silt (Undifferentiated) And Sand(Undifferentiated)). History

Seat of the Lordship of the Isles in the medieval period, ruled from Finlaggan. Whisky distilling has shaped its modern economy.

How to reach it

CalMac ferries from Kennacraig to Port Ellen and Port Askaig. Loganair flies into Islay Airport from Glasgow.

Accommodation

Hotels in Bowmore, Port Ellen and Port Charlotte, plus distillery-owned guest houses and cottages.
