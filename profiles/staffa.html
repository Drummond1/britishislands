Staffa

Uninhabited basalt island famed for Fingal's Cave, whose hexagonal columns inspired Mendelssohn's overture.

Names

Scottish Gaelic: Stafa; Irish: Stafa; Welsh: Stafa

Geography

Formed by the same Palaeocene lava flow as Northern Ireland's Giant's Causeway. Fingal's Cave is 20 m high and 70 m deep.

History

Sir Joseph Banks 'rediscovered' Staffa for science in 1772; Queen Victoria, Walter Scott, Wordsworth and Verne all visited.

How to reach it

Day boat trips from Mull (Fionnphort or Ulva Ferry) and from Iona, weather permitting.

Accommodation

None — visited as a day trip only.
