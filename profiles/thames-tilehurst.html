Tilehurst

Tilehurst is a village on the western outskirts of the town of Reading in the English county of Berkshire, England. It is partly within the boundaries of the Borough of Reading, a part sometimes referred to as Tilehurst Within, and partly in the district of West Berkshire, referred to as Tilehurst W
