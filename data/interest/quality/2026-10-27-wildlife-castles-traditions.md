## Rejections

### Local traditions

- **Manx kippers, Isle of Man.** The Culture of the Isle of Man article lists them as a commercial delicacy smoked in Peel. It does not describe a custom, festival, dance, or craft.
- **Oie'l Verrey, Isle of Man.** The carol sentences do not name the Isle of Man, and the opening sentences do not name Oie'l Verrey, so no sentence places the custom on the island.
- **Kishie, Mainland (Shetland).** The article calls kishie-making native to the Shetland Islands. The summary has no sentence that places the craft on Mainland.
- **Shetland Wool Week, Mainland (Shetland).** The article places the workshops in venues across Shetland, not on Mainland.
- **Shetland lace, Mainland (Shetland).** The lace knitting article describes a Shetland tradition. No sentence places the craft on Mainland.
- **Reestit mutton, Mainland (Shetland).** The summary says the cure is traditional to the Shetland Islands. It does not include a sentence that places it on Mainland. A later sentence mentions a Lerwick butcher, and that sentence is not in the summary.

### Island castles

- **Lambay Castle, Lambay Island.** The article calls it a late-16th-century fort, or a small blockhouse, which Lutyens rebuilt as a private family residence. Forts and country houses do not count. The photograph is of that building, and the island id is valid.

### Unusual wildlife

- **Grey seal, Monach Islands.** The summary says the islands are of special interest for their grey seal population. It does not call the colony a principal stronghold, and the grey seal is not endemic or named for the islands. The next sentence does call it one of the largest colonies, but it also gives a count of seals coming ashore, which must not be stored.
- **Northern gannet, Sùla Sgeir.** The article says the island is best known for gannets and that its name means gannet skerry. The species is not endemic, not named for the island, and the article does not call this colony a principal stronghold. A breeding-pair count later in the article was not stored and must not be added. The same title is already accepted on a different island, so this is not a duplicate key.
