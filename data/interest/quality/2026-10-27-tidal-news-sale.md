## Rejections

### Tidal islands

- **Keallasay More.** The photograph is of Ceallasaigh Beag from the air. Ceallasaigh Mòr is only a clouded edge of that frame, so the picture is not of Keallasay More or of the tidal link.
- **Innisidgen.** The photograph is a sign on St Mary's pointing to the Innisidgen burial chamber, with the mainland shore in the foreground. It is not a photograph of the tidal island or of the link that joins it to St Mary's.
- **Burntwick Island.** The Burntwick Island article says Stangate Creek separates the island from Chetney Marshes, and that creeks across the marsh split it into pieces at high tide. It does not say a drying link joins the island to the mainland, or to another island, and is covered at high tide.
