Whisky islands

18 islands have a whisky or whiskey record a source places on the island: 30 distillery entries, 13 silent stills, 12 stories, and 2 that are named but not yet in production. A still is listed only when the source names the island.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

Rows cover a working distillery, a closed still, a story, or a planned still. The page does not list every bottle, brand, or bar. Islay, the Hebrides, Orkney, and other isles use the same cited rows.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Whisky islands”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/whisky-distilleries/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

Questions

Which Scottish islands make whisky?

The distillery rows on this page. An island appears only where a source places a still, a silent distillery, or a whisky story on it.

Are closed distilleries included?

Yes. 13 silent stills are listed separately from the 30 distillery entries.

Is Irish whiskey included?

Only when a source places the whiskey on an island in this atlas.
