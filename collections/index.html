Find My Island

Island guides

Lists you can cite: a castle on the island, a whisky still, a tidal crossing, a book set there. Each guide says what was left out, and links the source.

Guides

  • Islands with castlesIslands in Britain and Ireland with a castle on the island itself. The mainland, Martello towers, brochs, and country houses are left out.
  • Whisky islandsIslands with a whisky distillery, a silent still, or a whisky story a cited source places on that island.
  • Tidal islandsIslands joined to other land at low tide and cut off at high tide. No crossing times.
  • Islands for saleIslands in Britain and Ireland with a broker listing. The link leaves this site. Check the broker before treating a listing as current.
  • Rare wildlifeRare, endemic, or remarkable species a source places on an island in Britain or Ireland. No nest sites or colony coordinates.
  • Books set on islandsNovels, memoirs, and story collections whose source sets the book on an island in Britain or Ireland.
  • Island traditionsFestivals, dances, food, and crafts a source places on an island in Britain or Ireland.
  • Film, art and musicFilms, artworks, and music a cited source connects to an island in Britain or Ireland.
