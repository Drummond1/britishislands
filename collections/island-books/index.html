Books set on islands

22 islands are the setting of a novel, memoir, or story collection in this guide — 39 books. A book is included only when its article sets the story on that island.

How this list is made

“Inspired by”, “based on”, and “filmed on” are not enough. The sentence under a title is the one that places the book on the island.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Books set on islands”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/island-books/, retrieved 2026-10-26.
