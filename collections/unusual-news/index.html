Unusual news

50 islands where a report records something strange — 54 stories. Each note is a short account of the event, and the link goes to the article. The picture is of the place or the thing the report is about.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

A story is kept when the report places the event on that island. The wording here is not the article. Great Britain and Ireland are not listed. Private addresses and grave locations are not published.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Unusual news”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/unusual-news/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

Questions

What counts as unusual news?

A reported event on that island: a declaration of independence, a mystery, names that appeared, or another one-off.

Are the articles copied here?

No. Each note is a short original account, with a link to the report.

How many stories are listed?

54 stories across 50 islands.

The islands

Eigg

Scotland · 2 cited

  • The People's Republic of Eigg — In 1997 the residents of Eigg bought their island after years of dispute with absentee landlords.
  • A jog over a stegosaur — A researcher jogging the foreshore of Eigg ran over a bone set in a boulder, the first dinosaur fossil found on the island.

Gruinard Island

Scotland · 2 cited

  • Declared free of anthrax — Wartime anthrax tests left Gruinard Island closed for 48 years.
  • Fire on the anthrax island — In March 2022 a fire burned from one end of Gruinard Island to the other.

Stronsay

Scotland · 2 cited

  • The beast on the rocks — In September 1808 a carcass the islanders did not recognise washed onto the rocks at Rothiesholm Head on Stronsay.
  • A walrus under the ramp — In April 2026 the pier master on Stronsay saw a walrus as the ferry came in, and it settled under the linkspan.

Scotland · 2 cited

  • A new breed of cattle — The last residents left Swona in 1974 and the cattle stayed.
  • Trainers in the post — In September 2025 a parcel addressed to Swona arrived in the post: a pair of trainers, for an island whose only inhabitants are feral cattle.
