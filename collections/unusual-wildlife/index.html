Unusual wildlife

23 islands with a rare, endemic, or otherwise remarkable species a source places there — 30 species in all. The page names the island and the species. It does not give nest sites or advice on how to visit.

How this list is made

A species is listed only when the source paragraph names the island. Colony coordinates, nest counts, and visit instructions are not published.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Unusual wildlife”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/unusual-wildlife/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

Jersey

Crown Dependency · 2 cited

  • Jersey orchid — A. laxiflora is common in Normandy and Brittany (France), but in the United Kingdom it is represented only on the Channel Islands, where in Jersey it is called Jersey orchid and in Guernsey it is called Loose Flowered orchid .
  • western green lizard — All are protected species, with Jersey being the only native habitat in the British Isles for the green lizard, wall lizard, spiny toad and agile frog.

Lundy

England · 2 cited

  • Lundy cabbage — Coincya wrightii, known as Lundy cabbage, is a species of primitive brassicoid, endemic to the island of Lundy off the southwestern coast of England, where it is sufficiently isolated to have formed its own species, with its endemic phytophagous beetles.
  • Lundy cabbage flea beetle — Psylliodes luridipennis, commonly known as the Lundy cabbage flea beetle or the bronze Lundy cabbage flea beetle, is a species of flea beetle endemic to the island of Lundy, where it lives and feeds upon the endemic Lundy cabbage (Coincya wrightii).
