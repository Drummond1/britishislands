Rare wildlife

36 islands with a rare, endemic, or otherwise remarkable species a source places there — 55 species in all. The page names the island and the species. It does not give nest sites or advice on how to visit.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

A species is listed only when the source paragraph names the island. Colony coordinates, nest counts, and visit instructions are not published.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Rare wildlife”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/unusual-wildlife/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

Questions

Which British and Irish islands have rare wildlife?

The 36 islands on this page, covering 55 cited species.

Are breeding sites shown?

No. Nest sites and colony coordinates are not on this page.

Is every seabird colony included?

No. Only a species a source singles out on that island.
