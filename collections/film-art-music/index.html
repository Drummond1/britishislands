Film, art and music

110 islands tied to a film, an artwork, or a piece of music — 904 cited works (545 artworks, 286 music, 73 films). Each row names the work and the source that connects it to the island.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

A work is kept when the source connects it to that island. A shared name, a portrait of someone who once visited, or a picture of a different place is left out.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Film, art and music”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/film-art-music/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

12 rows were left off this page because the cited source does not place them on that island.

Questions

Which films are connected to British and Irish islands?

The film rows on this page. 73 films are cited, alongside 545 artworks and 286 pieces of music.

Why is a painting listed under an island?

Because the source says the work depicts that island, or files it with art of that island.
