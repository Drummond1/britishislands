How this list is made

Monastic pilgrimages, island breeds, and a bare legend are not listed. Each custom needs a source sentence that describes the practice and names the island.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Island traditions”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/local-traditions/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

Questions

What island traditions are recorded here?

72 customs on 35 islands: festivals, dances, food, and crafts the source places on that island.

Are island breeds included?

No. Breeds, pilgrimages, and a legend with no described custom are left out.

Where is Up Helly Aa?
