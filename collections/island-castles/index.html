Islands with castles

85 islands in Britain and Ireland have a castle the sources place on the island itself — 108 castles in all. The mainland of Great Britain and the island of Ireland are not listed. Martello towers, brochs, and country houses are left out.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

A castle is kept only when Wikidata, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, Canmore, the National Heritage List for England, Cadw, or the Archaeological Survey of Ireland places it on that island. The sentence under a name is the source line that makes the link.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Islands with castles”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/island-castles/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

5 rows were left off this page because the cited source does not place them on that island.

Questions

How many islands in Britain and Ireland have a castle?

This guide lists 85 islands and 108 castles. It counts a castle on an island, not a castle on the mainland of Britain or Ireland.

Are brochs and Martello towers included?

No. Martello towers, brochs, and country houses are left out.

Where does the list come from?

From cited records in Wikidata, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, and the national heritage lists. Each entry links to its source.
