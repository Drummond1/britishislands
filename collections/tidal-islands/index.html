Tidal islands

64 islands that are joined to other land at low tide and cut off at high tide, taken from a published list of tidal islands. This is not every island the atlas marks as tidal, and it does not give crossing times.

Open this list on the map → All islands

How this list is made

Each island is one the English Wikipedia article on tidal islands names, and that this atlas can place. A permanent causeway, a footbridge, or a name that is not an island in the atlas stays off the list.

The sentence under a title is taken from the linked source. Cite this page as Find My Island, “Tidal islands”, https://www.findmyisland.com/collections/tidal-islands/, retrieved 2026-10-26.

2 rows were left off this page because the cited source does not place them on that island.

Questions

What is a tidal island?

Land you can reach on foot at low tide, and that is surrounded by water at high tide.

How many tidal islands are listed here?

This guide lists 64. A broader tidal tag in the atlas is not used for this page.

Does this page say when it is safe to cross?

No. It does not give crossing times.
